# 3 Delta Dental - Sitemap & Design Strategy

## Executive Summary

Based on comprehensive competitor analysis of 10 major dental supply distributors, this document outlines the complete sitemap and conversion-focused design strategy for 3deltadental.com.

**Key Findings from Competitor Research:**
- **Search-first design** is universal across successful B2B dental sites
- **SKU visibility** on every product card/listing is critical
- **Trust signals** (TGA approval, certifications, stock status) drive B2B conversions
- **Dual navigation paths** (browse by category + search by SKU) serve different buyer behaviors
- **3 Delta Dental's unique advantages**: TGA approval, European brand focus, specialist expertise

## Homepage Design Strategy

**Current State:** Generic hero + category grid + featured products

**Recommended Structure:**

```
1. Search Hero Section
   - Heading: "Professional Dental Lab Supplies"
   - Large search bar with SKU support
   - Popular searches displayed

2. Trust Bar
   - TGA Approved | European Brands | 37+ Years Expertise | 24/7 Support
   - Dark blue background (#003B56), white text

3. Category Grid (8-12 categories)
   - Replace emojis with professional icons/images
   - Show product counts
   - Link to collections

4. Featured Products (8 products)
   - SKU visible on cards
   - Quick add-to-cart
   - Price excl GST

5. Brand Showcase
   - European manufacturer logos
   - "Official Distributor" messaging

6. Clearance Banner
   - Gold accent (#FED440)
   - Creates urgency

7. Newsletter Signup
```

**Why This Works:**
- Matches patterns used by Henry Schein, Zahn Dental, DHP Supply
- Search-first approach serves professional buyers
- Trust signals above the fold build credibility
- Multiple paths to products (search, categories, featured)

## Key Differentiators vs Competitors

### What No Competitor Does Well:

1. **TGA Approval Prominence**
   - No Australian competitor highlights TGA status visually
   - Opportunity: Add TGA badges on every product
   - Create dedicated TGA compliance page

2. **European Manufacturing Narrative**
   - Competitors mention brands but don't explain quality significance
   - Opportunity: Use country flags, "Made in Europe" messaging
   - Position European origin as premium signal

3. **Specialist Focus**
   - Most competitors carry 10,000+ products (generalists)
   - 3 Delta Dental: 699 carefully curated products (specialist)
   - Messaging: "Specialists in nano acrylics, zirconia, and advanced materials"
